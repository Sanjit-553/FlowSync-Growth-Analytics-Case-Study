# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Title

FlowSync Growth Analytics Case Study

---

## 1. Executive Summary

FlowSync is a B2B SaaS platform that provides project management and workflow automation solutions for organizations of varying sizes.

During 2024 and 2025, FlowSync experienced strong customer acquisition growth. However, revenue growth has not increased at the same rate. Leadership believes there may be inefficiencies in customer acquisition, product adoption, retention, and expansion revenue generation.

This project aims to analyze customer behavior, subscription trends, product usage, and marketing performance to identify opportunities for sustainable growth.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Although customer acquisition continues to increase, several business concerns have emerged:

- Revenue growth is slowing.
- Customer churn remains a concern.
- Marketing efficiency is unclear.
- Product adoption varies significantly across customers.
- Expansion revenue opportunities may be underutilized.

Leadership requires data-driven insights to determine the primary drivers of growth and identify areas for improvement.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

The objectives of this analysis are:

- Evaluate customer acquisition effectiveness.
- Identify drivers of customer activation.
- Understand factors influencing retention and churn.
- Analyze product feature adoption.
- Measure marketing channel performance.
- Identify opportunities to increase recurring revenue.
- Provide actionable recommendations to support business growth.

---

## 5. Business Questions

### Acquisition

- Which acquisition channels generate the highest-value customers?
- Which channels provide the best return on investment?

### Activation

- What behaviors indicate successful customer activation?
- Which customer segments activate most effectively?

### Retention

- What factors contribute to customer churn?
- Which customer segments have the highest retention rates?

### Product

- Which product features are most commonly adopted?
- Does feature adoption influence retention?

### Revenue

- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- What factors drive expansion revenue?

---

## 6. Scope

### Included

- Customer acquisition analysis
- Subscription analysis
- Revenue analysis
- Product usage analysis
- Retention analysis
- Marketing performance analysis
- Support performance analysis

### Excluded

- Engineering metrics
- Infrastructure metrics
- Security metrics
- Internal HR metrics

---

## 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Identify key drivers of customer growth.
- Identify key drivers of customer churn.
- Measure marketing channel effectiveness.
- Measure product adoption impact.
- Provide recommendations to improve revenue growth.
- Provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

- ## 9. Assumptions and Constraints

### Assumptions

- Customer acquisition data is available across all marketing channels.
- Product usage events are accurately captured.
- Subscription and revenue data are available for all customers.
- Customer support interactions are linked to customer accounts.

### Constraints

- The dataset is a simulated SaaS business environment.
- Results are intended for analytical and educational purposes.
- Findings are based on available historical data and may not represent future outcomes.
